Deterministic Engineering + AI Insights

visionIQ avoids unconstrained LLM calls. Deterministic mathematical filters eliminate hallucinations and reduce response times from 120 seconds to sub-second responses.

STEP 01
explore

Haversine Spatial Filtering

Java spatial calculations compute great-circle distances across 100+ cities to isolate 3–5 viable transit candidates prior to any AI call.

Contextual Cargo Mapping

Custom cargo descriptions (e.g. "Handmade Rajasthani Blue Pottery") are contextually parsed by Gemini to assign proper transport handling classes.

Scoring formulas balance Speed, Reliability, Cost, and Risk Factor to present transparent, explainable recommendations.
