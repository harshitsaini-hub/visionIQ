System Architecture

The Hybrid AI Advantage

visionIQ rejects unconstrained LLM calls. Deterministic mathematical filters protect against hallucinations and slashes latency from 120 seconds to sub-second responses.

STEP 01
explore

Haversine Spatial Filtering

Java-based spatial algorithms compute great-circle distances across 100+ cities to isolate 3–5 viable transit candidates prior to any AI invocation.

STEP 02
psychology

Gemini Cargo Classification

Custom cargo declarations (e.g. "Handmade Rajasthani Blue Pottery") are contextually parsed by Gemini to assign proper transport packaging requirements.

STEP 03
cloud_sync

Live Weather Radar

Real-time OpenWeather telemetry along transit corridor stops identifies localized disruptions, rain hazards, and seasonal transit delays.

STEP 04
tune

Multi-Objective Carrier Ranking

Deterministic scoring formulas balance Speed, Reliability, Cost, and Risk Score to present transparent, explainable recommendations.
