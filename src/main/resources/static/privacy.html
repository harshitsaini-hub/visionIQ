Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | visionIQ Platform

1. Introduction

visionIQ ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how operational route telemetry, pincode inputs, and cargo parameters are processed within our AI decision-support platform.

2. Data We Process

When executing route optimization analyses, visionIQ processes operational freight metadata:

  • Consignment Inputs: Origin hub pincode, destination hub pincode, cargo classification strings, priority requirements, and handling flags (fragile, perishable).
  • System Diagnostics: Session execution duration, API latency benchmarks, and anonymous network diagnostics.

3. Third-Party Cognitive & Environmental Services

visionIQ integrates with enterprise-grade external APIs:

  • Google Gemini AI: Freeform cargo descriptions and candidate hub arrays are processed contextually to determine freight classes and logical transit corridors.
  • OpenWeather API: Geographic latitude and longitude coordinates along transit corridors are queried to retrieve real-time weather alerts and precipitation telemetry.

4. Data Retention & Security

visionIQ does not persist private payment details or personally identifying user addresses in permanent databases. All queries are held in transient session state to generate real-time recommendations and benchmarks.

5. Contact

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact our team at privacy@visioniq.ai.
